@Cacheable in Spring Boot ☕

Stop running the same slow query again and again.

✅ @EnableCaching turns caching on
✅ @Cacheable stores a method's result by its arguments
✅ @CacheEvict removes entries to keep data fresh
✅ Calling the method from the same class skips the cache
