Floating-Point Precision in Java ☕

0.1 + 0.2 is not 0.3 in Java, and it is not a bug.

✅ double stores binary approximations, so many decimals are slightly off
✅ Never compare doubles with ==, use a small tolerance (epsilon)
✅ Use BigDecimal for money and exact decimal math
✅ Build BigDecimal from a String, and compare with compareTo()
