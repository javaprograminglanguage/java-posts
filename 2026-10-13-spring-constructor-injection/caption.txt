Constructor Injection in Spring Boot ☕

Stop putting @Autowired on fields. Ask for your dependencies in the constructor instead.

✅ Fields can be final, so your beans are immutable
✅ Unit test with plain "new", no Spring context needed
✅ Dependencies are visible right in the constructor
✅ A single constructor needs no @Autowired
