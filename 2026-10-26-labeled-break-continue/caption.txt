Labeled break & continue in Java ☕

Need to escape two nested loops at once? A label does it.

✅ A plain break exits only the innermost loop
✅ break outer; exits the loop named outer
✅ continue outer; jumps to the next outer iteration
✅ Often, extracting a method with return is cleaner
