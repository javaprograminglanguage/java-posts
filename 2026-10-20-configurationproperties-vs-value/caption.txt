@ConfigurationProperties vs @Value ☕

Still injecting every property with @Value? Spring Boot has a cleaner way.

✅ @Value is great for one-off values and supports SpEL
✅ @ConfigurationProperties binds a whole prefix to a record or class
✅ You get type conversion, validation and relaxed binding for free
✅ Register it with @ConfigurationPropertiesScan
