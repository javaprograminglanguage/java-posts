The JPA N+1 Problem ☕

You ran 1 query. Hibernate ran 101.

✅ @OneToMany is lazy by default
✅ Looping and calling getBooks() runs one extra query per author
✅ Fix it with join fetch in a @Query
✅ Or add @EntityGraph to the repository method
