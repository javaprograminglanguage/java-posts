Java volatile keyword ☕

Your thread keeps running even after you set the flag to false? 🛑

✅ A plain boolean flag may never be seen by another thread
✅ volatile makes every write visible to all threads
✅ volatile does not make count++ atomic
✅ Use AtomicInteger or synchronized for counters
