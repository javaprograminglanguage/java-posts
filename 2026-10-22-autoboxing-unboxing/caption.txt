Autoboxing & Unboxing in Java ☕

Integer is not int, and mixing them can bite you.

✅ Autoboxing turns int into Integer, unboxing turns it back
✅ Collections like List<Integer> can only hold wrapper objects
✅ Unboxing a null Integer throws a NullPointerException
✅ Prefer primitives in loops to avoid hidden object creation
