Generics Wildcards in Java ☕

Why isn't List<Integer> a List<Number>? A favorite interview question.

✅ Generics are invariant: Integer is a Number, but the lists are unrelated
✅ ? extends T is for reading (the producer)
✅ ? super T is for writing (the consumer)
✅ Remember PECS: Producer Extends, Consumer Super
