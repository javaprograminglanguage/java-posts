Stack vs Heap in Java ☕

Where does your data actually live in the JVM?

✅ Stack: one per thread, holds method frames, local variables and references
✅ Heap: shared by all threads, holds every object and is garbage collected
✅ Endless recursion → StackOverflowError
✅ Too many live objects → OutOfMemoryError
