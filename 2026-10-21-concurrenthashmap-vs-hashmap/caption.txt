ConcurrentHashMap vs HashMap ☕

HashMap is not thread-safe. Here is what to use instead.

✅ Sharing a HashMap between threads can lose updates or corrupt data
✅ ConcurrentHashMap is safe for concurrent use without locking the whole map
✅ Use merge() and putIfAbsent() instead of check-then-put
✅ ConcurrentHashMap does not allow null keys or null values
