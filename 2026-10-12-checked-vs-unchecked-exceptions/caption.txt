Checked vs Unchecked Exceptions in Java ☕

A classic interview question, explained in 7 slides.

✅ Checked exceptions: the compiler forces you to handle or declare them
✅ Unchecked exceptions extend RuntimeException and usually mean a bug
✅ Use checked when the caller can recover
✅ Your custom exception's parent class decides which kind it is
