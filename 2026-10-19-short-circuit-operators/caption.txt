&& vs & in Java ☕

Two ANDs, but only one is lazy. Do you know the difference?

✅ && and || stop as soon as the result is known
✅ & and | on booleans always run both sides
✅ Use && to guard against NullPointerException
✅ On ints, & and | work bitwise
