List.of vs Arrays.asList in Java ☕

Is your List really immutable? Not always!

✅ List.of() is fully immutable and rejects nulls
✅ Arrays.asList() has a fixed size, but set() works
✅ Collections.unmodifiableList() is only a read-only view
✅ List.copyOf() gives you a real snapshot
